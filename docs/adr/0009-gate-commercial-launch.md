# Gate commercial launch on proof and legal review

The route proof and first ten-person pilot are private and unpaid. The project does not advertise, sell subscriptions, promise an SLA, or treat approximate Xray counters as billing evidence. External tester admission and any paid launch require the accepted technical gates, DigitalOcean account and acceptable-use confirmation, written tester terms, and qualified legal advice for the operator's jurisdiction covering licensing, international connectivity, identity, retention, privacy, payments, and abuse response.
