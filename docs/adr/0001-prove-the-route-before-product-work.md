# Prove the route before product work

The proof of concept will first validate a direct Tokyo route from the primary Hangzhou China Telecom 5G connection, then validate an Xray tunnel through v2rayNG. Control-plane installation and Android customization stay out of scope until both checks pass. A relay will be considered only if direct routing fails. This phase gate avoids building product features around a network path that may be unusable or exceed the total infrastructure ceiling of about USD 20 per month.
